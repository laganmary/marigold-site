Privacy policy

Your money stays yours.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

In short: Marigold has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything you enter is stored on your device. The only thing the app fetches from the internet is currency exchange rates.

This policy explains how the Marigold app for Android (“Marigold”, “the app”) handles information. Marigold is made by Maria Lagan (“I”, “me”), an individual entrepreneur registered in Georgia. If you have any questions, write to howzit@laganm.art.

What I collect

Nothing. I don't run servers for Marigold, and the app doesn't send me your accounts, transactions, goals, budgets, notes, name or any other personal or financial information. There's no sign-up, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Marigold — your name for greetings, accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring payments and settings — is stored locally in the app's private storage on your phone. You can view, change or delete it at any time in the app.

Exchange rates

To convert amounts between currencies, Marigold downloads public exchange-rate files from the jsDelivr content delivery network (the open-source “currency-api” dataset). These requests only contain a currency code and a date. Like any website or service you connect to, the network provider can see your device's IP address when it delivers the file. No personal or financial information from the app is included in these requests.

Bank statement import

When you import a bank statement, the file is read and processed entirely on your device. It isn't uploaded anywhere and no AI services are used. If you choose “Send me the file structure”, the app prepares a summary with only column names and row counts — never your transactions — and you decide whether and how to share it using your phone's share menu.

Backups and exports

  • Marigold backups and CSV exports are files that you create and save where you choose: on your phone, in a cloud drive, or anywhere else. You can protect backups with a password (AES encryption). That password isn't stored or recoverable by me.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may automatically back up the app's data to your own Google account, as it does for other apps. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy, and I have no access to it. You can manage it in your phone's settings.

App lock and fingerprint

Your PIN and recovery code are stored on your device in a protected form. If you unlock with your fingerprint or face, this is handled by Android; Marigold never receives your biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders (for example, about recurring payments or backups) are created on your device. No notification data is sent to me.

Payments

Marigold is free. If the app offers optional in-app purchases, such as a way to say thanks, they are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. I don't receive your card or payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to download exchange rates.
  • Notifications — for the reminders you turn on.
  • Vibration — for small haptic feedback.

Files you import or back up are accessed only through the system file picker, and only the files you choose.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. Since I don't hold any of your data, there is nothing to delete on my side.

Children

Marigold isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects what happens with your data, it will also be mentioned in the app's update notes.
