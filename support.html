Howzit! How can I help?

Found a bug, have an idea, or need help with something in Marigold? Write to me — I read every message.

laganm.sa@gmail.com

Tip for bug reports: tell me your phone model, Android version and what you were doing when it happened. A screenshot helps a lot. Please don't send your backup files or bank statements — I never need your financial data.

Quick answers

  • Where is my data? Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • Moving to a new phone? In Marigold, go to Settings → Backup & export, save a backup, then restore it on the new phone from the welcome screen.
  • Forgot your PIN? Use your fingerprint or the 12-character recovery code you saved when you turned on the lock.
  • My bank statement won't import. Export it as Excel (.xlsx) or CSV from your bank. PDF statements can't be read yet.
